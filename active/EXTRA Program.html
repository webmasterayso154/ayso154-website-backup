Program Overview

Purpose

AYSO EXTRA is an internal gaming-circuit program that offers players a higher-level opportunity to refine their skills in a more competitive playing environment. It is designed to provide a more challenging level of play for those players who possess the appropriate interest, skills, and abilities to enhance their individual growth through the game of soccer.

Why AYSO EXTRA?

  • Backed by a National Soccer Organization
  • Extremely reduced player fees (when compared to standard club soccer)
  • Playing time per game is guaranteed

About the Program

EXTRA is committed to developing a world-class competitive soccer program focused on providing a challenging environment and enhancing training for our dedicated soccer enthusiasts.

  • Competition: In the Fall, EXTRA teams will play against other EXTRA teams within Section 11 in the Southern California Extra Development Soccer League (SCEDSL).
  • Tryouts: All interested players attend a tryout in the Fall and/or Spring. Players are evaluated by an independent assessment team.
  • Selection: Players chosen to participate will be notified shortly after tryouts. Players who are not chosen will be placed on a core team in the Fall season.
Learn More About the League Visit the SCEDSL website for details on Sections, Areas, and Regions involved.
Visit AYSO SCEDSL Website

Player & Parent Commitment

Participation in the EXTRA program requires a higher level of commitment. The season requires a full-year commitment running from Summer through the following Spring. Attendance at all team functions (practices, games, tournaments) is expected and critical to a successful season.

Strict Commitment Policy The EXTRA program demands more time and needs to be the player's primary sport commitment. Players in the EXTRA program cannot play in another competitive soccer program (i.e. Club) or Core.

Extra Coach Requirements

  • Experience: Recommendation of at least 3 seasons coaching experience (Fall and Spring Select count as separate seasons). Preferably in Region 154.
  • Certification: Must have completed the AYSO coaching certification equal to the age group you are applying for.
  • Referee: Must have at least a Regional referee certification.
  • Commitment: Provide at least a 1-year commitment to the Program.
  • Exclusivity: If you are selected as an EXTRA head coach, you are ineligible to be a head coach of any other team(s).
  • Representation: Represent Region 154 with dignity at all Regional events and at away games.
  • EPIC Buddy Program: Divisions U11 and older must participate in the EPIC Buddy program at least once during the fall season.

Required Meetings

Attendance at the following meetings is mandatory:

  1. Welcome Meeting: Summer (Uniform orders & upcoming season).
  2. Section EXTRA Meeting: Late Summer.
  3. Fall Season Wrap-Up: Winter (Dependent on SCEDSL schedule).
  4. Tryouts: Spring.
