Region Registration Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand that family circumstances can change. Please review the deadlines and instructions below before submitting a cancellation or refund request.

Looking for the Refund Request Form?

Download the form, complete it, and email it to the Registrar, Regional Commissioner, and Treasurer.

📄 Download Refund Form (PDF)
Waitlist Cancellation If your player is currently on the waitlist, you have not paid registration fees yet and do not need to submit the refund form. Email our volunteer Registrar at Registrarayso154@gmail.com to have your player removed from the waitlist.

Policy Deadlines & Conditions

  • Refund requests must be received by July 31, 2026, minus the non-refundable $25 National AYSO Membership Fee charged by AYSO National. 
  • Refunds will not be issued for requests received after August 1, 2026.
  • All refund requests must be emailed by the applicable deadline to:
    • Registrar: Registrarayso154@gmail.com
    • Regional Commissioner: RCayso154@gmail.com
    • Treasurer: Treasurerayso154@gmail.com
  • Approved refunds will normally be credited to the original form of payment. If the original payment method cannot be refunded, the Region will mail a check.
  • Refunds will be issued within four weeks of the emailed request. If you do not receive an email confirmation within 10 business days, resend the request to both the RC and Treasurer.
  • Refunds cannot be granted because of a practice-schedule conflict. Practice schedules are determined by the volunteer Head Coach and Regional Scheduler. The only way to guarantee control over a team's practice schedule is to volunteer as a Head Coach.
  • Refunds cannot be granted because a player was not placed with a requested teammate or coach, or because a player was placed with a particular teammate or coach. AYSO Region 154 must uphold the AYSO philosophy of Balanced Teams.

Inclement Weather & Game Cancellation Policy

We understand that weather and field conditions can sometimes impact scheduled games. Our organization will make every reasonable effort to reschedule games canceled due to inclement weather or unsafe field conditions, when scheduling and field availability allow.

Please understand that game cancellations do not qualify for refunds, credits, or prorated registration fees. Registration fees cover the overall youth sports program and associated operating costs, not individual games or practices. By registering, participants acknowledge and agree that all registration fees are final and non-refundable, including in the event of weather-related cancellations or other circumstances beyond the organization’s control. Thank you for your understanding and for supporting our efforts to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all players.
