Guidelines for a Smooth Day

Player & Team Instructions

*Refer to the official Picture Day Flyer for complete rules. Key guidelines include:

  1. Arrive 30 minutes early. Plan to be at the grassy area outside the Community Center well before your scheduled time.
  2. Line up outside. Teams will not be allowed to line up inside the building. Players will be escorted in once the previous team has exited through a separate door.
  3. Wear sneakers or slides. For the safety of the facility, NO CLEATS or SHIN GUARDS are allowed inside the building.
  4. Team personnel only. Only the Coach, Assistant Coach, and Team Parent will be allowed inside with the players. All other parents must wait outside.
  5. Team photos are for the team. Only registered players and certified team personnel are permitted in the official team photo.
  6. Complete one form per player. A picture form for each player is mandatory, even if you are not ordering extra photos. We will provide these when your team lines up.
Your Basic Package is Included!
Your registration fees already include a basic photo package for each player: an 8x10 memory mate (with individual and team photos), wallet-sized photos, and a player button. Additional items can be ordered on the picture form.

Enjoy the Fun!

We'll have Kona Ice Truck, face painters, and inflatable games. Make a day of it!
