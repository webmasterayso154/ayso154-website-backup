Game Day Rules & Sideline Culture

The "LEFT" Rule for Sideline Seating For the safety and organization of the game, spectators must sit on the LEFT side of their team's Coach.

• Home Team: North or West side of the field.
• Visiting Team: South or East side of the field.
Zero Tolerance Policy
No yelling at, arguing with, or showing dissent or abuse toward referees, especially youth referees. They are children dedicating themselves to the game. Be nice.
Park Lexington Turf Field Rules
  • Water only on the turf. No Gatorade, sports drinks, soda, snacks, or food.
  • No equipment on turf: no wagons, EZ-Ups, tents, or chairs.
  • All spectators, players, and coaches must set up on the stadium side of the field.
The Why Behind Our 6-Goal Rule
We uphold the AYSO principle of Good Sportsmanship by asking coaches to manage the game to prevent blowouts past a 6-goal differential. Thank you for cheering on the good sportsmanship this rule promotes.

Practice Readiness & Maps

Player Drop-off & Pick-up

  • Players must not be left at the field without supervision by an AYSO Safe Haven certified adult.
  • Make direct contact with your coach or team parent during drop-off and pick-up.
  • Do not rely on your child to relay pick-up messages.

School District Facility Rules

  • No dogs on school fields, service animals excepted.
  • No smoking or alcohol on school property.
  • Do not use Day Care or school restrooms.
  • Pick up and properly dispose of all trash after practice.
Important Safety Protocol A coach or team parent must wait with any player until a responsible adult arrives for pick-up. The waiting adult must be the same gender as the player. If a parent/guardian cannot be contacted after approximately one hour, the volunteer must notify the local police department for assistance.
Parking Alert Only park in designated parking zones. Cypress PD will ticket vehicles parked in non-permitted parking areas. It is the responsibility of the driver to follow local laws and ordinances regarding proper parking requirements. Do not enter fields through the Lexington JHS gymnasium.

Field Setup & Takedown Procedures

  • Teams scheduled for the first game of the day are responsible for setting up goals, sandbags, and corner flags.
  • Teams scheduled for the last game of the day must return all equipment to the lockup and ensure the field is spotless.

2026 Referee & Volunteer Points System

Earning a spot in the Cypress Cup and/or Playoffs is a team effort that extends to volunteering! For divisions U8 through U19 & EXTRA, teams must earn a minimum of 17 points to qualify for post-season play.

🏁 Goal: 17 Points Minimum Required Out of 26 total possible points, teams must secure at least 17 to advance. Playground, U5, and U6 are not scored but are still expected to participate as an all-volunteer organization.
⬛ Referee Points (17 Max Possible)
+5
Certified Referees Earn 1 pt (up to 5 max) per certified referee assigned to your team and activated in Matchtrak.
+2
Early Sign-Up Bonus Earn 2 pts if all scheduled team games are filled with referees in Matchtrak before Sept 26.
+10
Complete Match Assignments Earn 1 pt (up to 10 max) per assignment completed on the field in full uniform with QR code scan.
🟩 Volunteer Points (9 Max Possible)
+2
Field Marshal Duty Earn 2 pts by having 2 trained volunteers complete 1 shift each (or 1 volunteer complete 2 shifts).
+5
Friday Night Field Set-Up Earn 5 pts for having volunteers present for ALL Friday night field set-ups during the season.
+2
Picture Picnic Day Earn 1 pt per volunteer (up to 2 max) for checking in and completing a 1-hour shift on Picture Day.

Parent Code of Conduct

AYSO is built around Everyone Plays, Balanced Teams, Open Registration, Positive Coaching, Good Sportsmanship, and Player Development.

AYSO Six Philosophies

  • Everyone Plays®
  • Balanced Teams
  • Open Registration
  • Positive Coaching
  • Good Sportsmanship
  • Player Development

Strictly Prohibited

  • No dogs, service animals excepted.
  • No drugs, tobacco, or alcohol at any AYSO event.

I understand and accept that if I violate this Code of Conduct, I may be asked to leave the game.
