For our Business & Community Sponsors

Sponsor Benefits

Your contribution of $150.00 makes a huge difference! As a thank you, sponsors receive:

  • A commemorative plaque with the team's picture.
  • Your business name featured on our website for the 2025 season.
  • Bonus: Teams with a 2nd or 3rd sponsor get a pizza party!  🍕🎉

Submission Guidelines

To ensure your sponsorship is processed correctly, please follow these guidelines when emailing your form to sponsorshipayso154@gmail.com.

  • Email Subject Line: Sponsorship for (Division) (Team's Last Name)
  • File Naming: (Division)_(Team's Last Name)_(Payment Type)
