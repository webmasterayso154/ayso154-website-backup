Season Orientation & Team Leader Resources

Welcome to the Fall 2026 Season! Whether you attended our live kickoff orientation or are stepping into a team leadership role post-meeting, this resource hub contains everything Head Coaches, Assistant Coaches, and Team Managers need to guide their team toward a smooth, fun, and compliant season.

Official Presentation & Briefing Materials

Review the full orientation presentation deck or listen to a quick audio summary before holding your first team practice or sending your initial family welcome email.

Download Fall 2026 Orientation Deck (PDF) Listen to 10-Minute Audio Overview

Key Takeaways for Coaches & Team Managers

A quick-reference summary of critical orientation topics:

1. AYSO Philosophies & Conduct Uphold our 6 Core Philosophies: Everyone Plays, Balanced Teams, Open Registration, Positive Coaching, Good Sportsmanship, and Player Development. Maintain a strict Kids Zone environment at all fields.
2. Volunteer Clearance (CVPA) All Head Coaches, Assistant Coaches, and Team Managers must complete background checks, Live Scan fingerprinting, Safe Haven, CDC Concussion, and Sudden Cardiac Arrest certifications before rosters are released.
3. Team Logistics & Sponsorships Team Managers manage family communications, collect team sponsorship forms ($150 minimum per team), and coordinate Picture Day logistics.
4. Game Day & Referee Points Coaches print official lineup cards from Sports Connect for every match. Divisions 10U–19U must accumulate team volunteer points (referees, field setup, field marshals) to qualify for end-of-season playoffs and the Cypress Cup.
Mandatory Pre-Season Action Required: Please review the complete presentation deck or audio overview with your coaching staff and Team Manager prior to your first team communication and initial practice session.

Practice & Facility Policies

Player Drop-off & Pick-up

  • Players must not be left at the field without supervision by an AYSO Safe Haven certified adult (Coach, Asst. Coach, or Team Manager).
  • If your child's certified team adult has not yet arrived, parents must wait with their child.
  • Ensure direct contact with your Coach or Team Manager when dropping off AND picking up your child.
Important Coach Safety Protocol: A Coach, Asst. Coach, or Team Manager must wait with any player until a parent or responsible adult arrives for pick-up. The waiting adult must be of the same gender as the player.

School District Rules for Facility Use

All coaches, parents, players, and guests must abide by the following school district rules at all times. Remember, these fields are a KIDS ZONE.

  • NO DOGS ALLOWED on school fields at any time (service animals excepted).
  • NO SMOKING or ALCOHOL is permitted on any school property.
  • NO urinating or defecating on school property or in surrounding neighborhoods.
  • DO pick up and properly dispose of all trash after every practice. Leave it better than you found it.
Lexington Park (Synthetic Field) Restrictions
  • Molded rubber cleats or turf shoes only (studs not to exceed 1-inch).
  • Water ONLY. No sports drinks or other liquids.
  • No sharp objects or straight-leg folding chairs that can penetrate the turf.
  • No stake umbrellas (must have a weighted stand).

Safety & Emergencies

Suspected Concussion Protocol Remove the player from participation immediately and do not allow same-day return. Notify the parent/guardian and follow the required Incident Report and Return-to-Play clearance process.
Respond Stop play, assess danger, and call 911 for life-threatening emergencies.
Notify Contact player’s parent/guardian and inform Region Safety Director.
Document Complete Incident Report for injuries, fighting, damage, or hospitalization.
Clear the Player Do not return player until required parent and medical forms are complete.

Goal Differential & Sportsmanship

Keep Goal Differential at Six or Fewer Coaches must make a genuine effort to avoid running up the score. Begin making tactical adjustments early rather than waiting until the match is out of balance.

Positive Adjustments Coaches Can Make

  • Focus on one player: Instruct the team to only pass to the least experienced player (who should be positioned at center mid-field). The team must work to earn the ball back and pass it right back to that one player.
  • Give developing players additional playing time and expanded responsibilities.
  • Move experienced players into unfamiliar positions (e.g., goalies play on the field).
  • Switch attacking and defensive players, or play a player down.
