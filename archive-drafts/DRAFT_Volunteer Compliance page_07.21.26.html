⚑ Complete Before Team Practice

Two Checkpoints to Know

Contingency means a required readiness checkpoint—not an estimated date or an automatic waiver.

Before Any Team Practice

First Practice Contingency

All required team volunteers must be in place, cleared for their roles, and released by Region 154 before the team practices.

Before the First Scheduled Game

First Game Contingency

The team must finish any remaining roster, referee, scheduling, or game-day requirements communicated by Region 154 before playing.

5 Role Training + Region Review

Finish Your Role Certification

  • Coaches: Complete the age-appropriate coach certification and any required in-person session.

  • Team Parents: Complete the screening and safety requirements assigned to the role.

Save completion certificates until every item appears in the official record.

Wait for the Release Message

Finishing your personal checklist does not release the team. Practice may begin only after Region 154 confirms that the entire team has met the First Practice Contingency.

Stuck? Check This First

Open only the item that matches your problem.

I did not receive the Sterling email.

Check spam or junk, confirm the email on your volunteer application, and make sure you selected Risk Status → Renew & Update. AYSO says the invitation normally arrives within about an hour.

Check the official Sterling status instructions.

My SafeSport course is missing or not syncing.

Use the SafeSport link provided in your Region 154 volunteer account, then complete the assigned course on your dashboard. Your name, date of birth, and email must match your AYSO record. Allow about 45 minutes after completion for the status to sync.

I completed training, but it still shows as missing.

Confirm the course is fully completed—not just enrolled or started. Save the certificate, then verify that the name and email on the training account match your volunteer application.

I am registering a youth referee.

Youth referees use a youth volunteer record and complete the youth safety and referee training assigned by AYSO. Adult Sterling, adult SafeSport, and adult Live Scan requirements do not apply to volunteers under 18.

Why can another team practice before ours?

Teams are released individually. Every required position on your team must be filled and cleared before Region 154 can release the team.
