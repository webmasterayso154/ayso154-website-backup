Which Rules Apply to My Match?

A referee may need to consider the IFAB Laws, AYSO modifications and approved competition instructions.

1
Immediate Priority

Player and Participant Safety

Address unsafe field conditions, dangerous equipment, injuries, severe weather and threatening behavior before continuing the match.

2
Foundation

Current IFAB Laws of the Game

The IFAB Laws provide the worldwide foundation for match officiating, referee authority, fouls, misconduct and restarts.

3
AYSO Application

AYSO National Rules and Regulations

AYSO modifies and supplements the Laws for youth participation, playing time, substitutions, equipment and player-development initiatives.

4
Assignment Instructions

Approved Region and Competition Guidance

Review the current division, program, field and competition instructions before the assignment.

5
Referee Responsibility

Apply Judgment Within the Laws

The referee uses knowledge, experience, game awareness and the spirit of the game while applying the approved rules.

A local instruction cannot create an unauthorized change to the Laws. When two sources appear to conflict, follow the approved competition instructions for the immediate match and ask the Regional Referee Administrator afterward.

High-Level Referee Summary

2026–2027 Laws of the Game Update

Use this section as an orientation. Review the complete Region 154 and AYSO update before applying a new procedure.

Law 3 — The Players

Team-Captain Guidelines

Competition Option

IFAB introduced guidance that competitions may use to limit certain approaches to the referee to team captains. AYSO does not automatically require all communication to occur only through captains.

Law 3 — Substitutions

Time-Limited Substitution Procedure

Review AYSO Application

A substituted player is expected to leave within the defined time. AYSO guidance explains how the procedure applies when monitored substitutions are used.

Law 5 — Injuries

Off-Field Treatment and Assessment

New Return Timing

In most cases, a player whose injury causes play to stop or who receives on-field assessment must leave and remain off for the prescribed period after play restarts.

Law 4 — Equipment

Safely Covered Non-Dangerous Items

Referee Safety Decision

Current guidance allows consideration of certain safely secured non-dangerous items. AYSO continues to treat jewelry as dangerous and not permitted.

Law 8 — Dropped Ball

Team That Had or Would Gain Possession

Restart Clarification

Outside the penalty area, the dropped ball may go to the team that had or clearly would have gained possession; otherwise the applicable fallback is used.

Law 12 — Goalkeeper Control

Eight Seconds and a Corner-Kick Sanction

Encourage Before Penalizing

The goalkeeper-control limit and referee countdown procedure have changed. The referee should use proactive communication before applying the new corner-kick sanction.

Law 12 — DOGSO Advantage

Goal Scored After Advantage

Disciplinary Change

When advantage is applied for an offence that would otherwise deny an obvious goal-scoring opportunity, the disciplinary outcome changes when a goal is scored.

Laws 15 and 16

Delayed Throw-Ins and Goal Kicks

Visual Countdown

When a restart is being deliberately delayed, the referee may begin the prescribed visual countdown. The resulting restart depends on whether it was a throw-in or goal kick.

These summaries are not a substitute for the official update. Review the complete AYSO presentation and Region 154 guidance, especially the exceptions, implementation notes and examples.

AYSO and Competition Guidance

Review the instructions for the specific division and competition instead of assuming every match uses the same format.

Match Format

Players, Field and Match Duration

Younger divisions may use smaller fields, fewer players, different ball sizes and shorter periods.

Participation

Playing Time and Substitutions

AYSO participation rules and the approved substitution system must be understood before the match begins.

Player Development

Heading Restrictions

Younger players are subject to heading restrictions. Review the current division and AYSO guidance before applying the restart.

Goalkeeper Development

Punt and Drop-Kick Restrictions

Certain younger divisions restrict goalkeeper punts and drop kicks. Confirm the applicable division and restart.

Build-Out Line

Possession and Offside Guidance

The Build-Out Line affects goalkeeper distribution, opponent positioning and the offside line in designated younger divisions.

Competition Differences

Core, EXTRA and Interleague

Programs may publish different approved competition instructions. Confirm the match listing and pregame guidance.

Do not invent a local rule during the match. When a procedure is unclear, use the approved competition instructions, discuss the issue with the referee crew and ask the RRA or mentor after the game.

Unsure About a Match Decision?

Record the Situation and Ask After the Match

Referees will encounter situations that are difficult to interpret in real time. Avoid an extended sideline debate. Make the best decision available, continue the match when safe and review the question afterward.

Include the division, competition, match time, player positions, sequence of events, decision made and restart used. Do not include unnecessary personal information.

Common Rules and Learning Questions

Rules & Learning FAQ

Which edition of the Laws should I use?

Use the current 2026/27 IFAB Laws together with the current AYSO and competition guidance.

When do the new Laws apply in Region 154?

Region 154 currently identifies August 1, 2026 as the effective date for the 2026–2027 changes.

Does every IFAB change apply directly to AYSO?

No. Review the AYSO annual update and National Rules for the approved youth-soccer application.

May Region 154 create any local rule it wants?

Local instructions must remain within the modifications permitted by IFAB, AYSO and the governing competition.

Where can I download the complete Laws?

Use the official IFAB document page or the official IFAB Laws app.

Where are AYSO-specific rules located?

Use the AYSO Reference Book, National Referee Program resources and current annual update.

Which divisions use the Build-Out Line?

Follow the current Region and competition instructions for the assigned division rather than assuming every younger division uses the same procedure.

Does the Build-Out Line affect offside?

Yes, in the divisions using the Build-Out Line. Review the current detailed guidance before the match.

May I use an old You Make the Call video?

Videos can help with learning, but confirm the Law year and current interpretation before relying on them.

What should I do when coaches cite different rules?

Avoid an extended debate. Apply the approved match guidance and review the issue with referee leadership afterward.

Where are game-day administrative procedures located?

MatchTrak, check-in, cards and reporting procedures are maintained on Game Day Tools.

How can I improve my rule knowledge?

Review current documents, attend annual training, discuss difficult situations and request mentoring.
