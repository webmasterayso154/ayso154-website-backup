Silent Auction & Remote Support

Support the Kids from Anywhere!

Our Silent Auction keeps player fees the lowest in the Area. Even if you cannot physically attend Picture Day, grandparents, out-of-town family, and local businesses can still participate and support our region.

  • Buy-It-Now Premium Baskets: Secure a high-value "Mega-Basket" immediately without having to place a bid or wait until the end of the day.
  • Virtual Baskets (Direct Donation): Want to support the kids without receiving a physical item? Sponsor a virtual basket as a direct monetary contribution to the region.
  • Proxy Pickup: For remote buyers purchasing physical baskets, simply list the Player Name and Division in the checkout notes so they can pick it up for you on Picture Day!

Team Manager Hub

The "One-Week Out" Form Checkpoint

To ensure a smooth "Fast Pass" experience for our players, Team Managers are responsible for ensuring 100% of their players have the Mandatory Photo Form filled out prior to arriving on Sunday.

  1. Download the Required Player Photo Form below.
  2. At your last practice before Picture Day, verify that every parent has completed the middle section of the form.
  3. Have blank copies on hand at practice for parents who forgot to print theirs.
  4. On Picture Day, players must have this physical form in-hand to give directly to the photographer.
