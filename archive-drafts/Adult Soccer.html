We know how busy life can get with work, school, family, and soccer. Thank you for volunteering with Cypress AYSO 154, but now it’s YOUR turn to play! 

Registration Requirements:  
1.     Must be a registered and active volunteer with AYSO 154 (coach, assistant coach, referee, board member, parent) during the fall 2024 season
2.     Current or previously had, a child registered in Cypress AYSO 154* 
* Exceptions to these requirements will be considered by the Regional Commissioner and the Adult League Coordinator on an individual basis. 

Seasons and Fee:
Our program is structured as a year-round registration that will cover you for three mini-seasons spanning in Spring 2025 through April 2025 . 
Fees: TBD

Location and Game Format:
During the season, we will hold games on Wednesday nights. Games will be in a Co-Ed Pick-Up Game format for 50 minutes. The game times may vary slightly based on the season. On game days, we will split into teams and play!  Ages 18+ and 40+. We will divide up in these age groups. Check website to register on the google doc. Games will be played in multiple sessions for 4-5 weeks each session.  

Depending on turnout we may choose to play small-sided games (on smaller fields for those of us that are endurance-challenged) or play full-sided games.

The Adult Soccer program is designed to be flexible to best accommodate our players skills and needs. This may include holding games at different times based on skill level. We may even switch things up throughout the season… one week a full-field 11 v 11 game and the next week break the field into three fields to play three different 4 v 4 ‘street-soccer’ style games.
