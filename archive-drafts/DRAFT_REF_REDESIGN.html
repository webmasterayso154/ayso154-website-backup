Prevent Conflicting Information

Page Ownership and Change Control

Each subject should have one owning page. Other pages may provide a short summary and link to the owner rather than copying the complete procedure.

Referee Central
Orientation, audience routing, high-level program model and links to specialized resources.
Become a Referee
Adult onboarding and preparation for an initial referee assignment.
Youth Referees
Youth registration, parent responsibilities, youth development and youth-referee protection.

Rules & Learning
Current Laws, local rule guidance, Build-Out Line and referee education.
Advancement & Paid Refereeing
AYSO advancement, mentoring and the separate paid licensing and association pathway.

Source-of-Truth Rule

When a policy, procedure, date, link or requirement changes, update the owning page first. Then search all seven pages for any summary that also needs revision.

Do not copy changing course dates, MatchTrak competition links, seasonal point totals or annual fees across several referee pages.

Recommended Publication Sequence

Publish the system only after page content, cross-page links and legacy-page decisions are coordinated.

1
Content Review

Vince Reviews All Seven Pages

Review pages in numerical order and record required changes using this hub and the internal comments on each page.

2
Decision Resolution

Resolve Cross-Page Procedures

Approve coverage, credit, card collection, youth, equipment, advancement and legacy-page decisions.

3
Final Revisions

Update the Affected Pages

Increase page versions, update version histories and keep unrelated pages unchanged when possible.

4
Technical QA

Test Desktop, Mobile and Links

Test every page, button, in-page anchor, visited state, email link and external resource.

5
Specialized Pages

Publish Pages 02 Through 07

Make the specialized pages publicly available before making Referee Central the primary landing page.

6
Landing Page

Publish Referee Central

Confirm that every destination is available, then publish Page 01 as the primary referee landing page.

7
Menu Migration

Configure the Text-Only Referees Menu

Add the approved page names and order. Do not add DRAFT_REF_REDESIGN to the public menu.

8
Legacy Migration

Redirect, Archive or Retain Old Pages

Follow the approved legacy map only after checking inbound links and preserved documents.

9
Search and Indexing

Enable Approved Public Settings

Enable indexing only for final public pages. Keep this project-review hub excluded.

10
Post-Publication QA

Test the Live Menu and Common User Paths

Test the site as a new adult, youth parent, current referee and mobile visitor before announcing the redesign.
