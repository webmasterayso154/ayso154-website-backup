Thank you for giving your time to help make game day safe, organized, and enjoyable for our players and families.

We know our AYSO parents are busy. Between work, school, family commitments, and multiple children’s activities, weekends can fill up quickly. That is why we are working to make Field Marshal volunteering as simple and flexible as possible.

AYSO is a volunteer-powered organization. Our coaches, referees, team parents, board members, and Field Marshals are community volunteers working together to create a positive soccer experience for our children. Every shift helps—and we are genuinely grateful for the time you give.

Four hours per team. Flexible ways to share them. Scheduled around your family whenever possible.

Choose What Works for Your Team

Each team completes a total of four Field Marshal volunteer hours per season. Your team may divide those hours in the way that works best:

Option 1: Share It Between Two

2 vols × 2 hours

Best for parents who prefer one continuous shift to fulfill their portion.

Option 2: Share It Between Four

4 vols × 1 hour

Best for busy families who can help, but for a shorter period of time.

Option 3: Mix and Match

1 two-hr + 2 one-hr

Your team may divide its four hours in the exact way that works best for you.

Your team provides four total hours. You decide how to share them.

What a Field Marshal Does

New to Field Marshal? No special soccer experience is needed. We will show you exactly where to check in, what to watch for, and who to contact if assistance is needed.

📱
1. Check In

Scan the check-in code and pick up your Field Marshal vest at the volunteer tent.

🚶
2. Be Visible

Stay visible, walk your assigned area, and be available to assist families.

👀
3. Observe and Report

Watch for safety concerns, prohibited activities, missing referees, or families needing assistance.

✅
4. Check Out

Complete the short report and hand the vest to the next volunteer.

Reassurance

Field Marshals observe and report. You are not expected to referee games, provide medical care, or handle serious conflicts by yourself. Board members are available on game days to provide support.

Plans Changed?

We understand that work, illness, family activities, and unexpected situations happen. Something unexpected came up? We understand.

Simply arrange for another qualified adult from your team to take the shift and update the sign-up information as soon as possible. The important thing is making sure the shift remains covered.

  • ⚽ The replacement must be an eligible adult.
  • ⚽ Enter the replacement’s name into the sign-up system.
  • ⚽ The original volunteer is responsible for ensuring coverage until confirmed.
  • ⚽ Last-minute issues should be communicated directly to the board member on duty.
