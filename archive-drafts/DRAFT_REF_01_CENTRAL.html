AYSO Volunteers and NOCRA Officials Working Together

How Region 154 Referee Coverage Works

Region 154 uses a blended model designed to improve game coverage while preserving meaningful opportunities for volunteer referees.

Center Referee Coverage

Region 154 Partners With NOCRA

NOCRA provides U.S. Soccer-credentialed Center Referees for many competitive games when an appropriate AYSO volunteer has not reserved the eligible Center position.

Volunteer Opportunities

AYSO Referees Remain Essential

Properly certified AYSO volunteers may serve as Center Referees and Assistant Referees based on the division, competition, assignment and referee’s current experience.

Team Support

Teams Help Provide Assistant Referees

Teams and Region volunteers continue to provide certified Assistant Referee coverage where required. Exact responsibilities vary by season and competition.

Confirm the current requirement for each match. Referee-crew requirements may differ by division, program, opponent and competition. Review Game Day Tools and the live MatchTrak listing when seasonal schedules are published.

From First Interest to Continued Development

The Region 154 Referee Journey

1
Register

Complete the Correct Volunteer Process

Adults complete the current adult application and compliance process. A parent or guardian completes the separate youth-volunteer process for referees under 18.

2
Train

Earn the Regional Referee Certification

Complete one of the approved entry-level training pathways and verify that the certification appears in the official record.

3
Prepare

Receive Equipment, Access and Support

Confirm uniform and equipment needs, scheduling access, current rules, field procedures and an assignment appropriate for your experience.

4
Officiate

Work With the Referee Crew

Arrive prepared, complete field check-in, support the referee team, protect the players and finish the required cards and reports.

5
Develop

Request Mentoring and Continue Learning

Build confidence through appropriate assignments, postgame feedback, annual education and formal or informal mentoring.

6
Advance

Choose Your Next Referee Goal

Continue serving at your current badge, pursue an AYSO upgrade, mentor other referees or explore the separate U.S. Soccer and NOCRA pathway.

Prepare Before Assignments Are Published

Referee Season Readiness

Referees can complete most preparation before MatchTrak competition schedules become available.

Check 01

Volunteer Eligibility

Confirm that the current application, screening and applicable safety requirements are complete.

Check 02

Certification

Confirm that referee courses and the correct badge appear in the official AYSOU record.

Check 03

Annual Rules

Review current AYSO Law changes, Region 154 procedures and applicable Build-Out Line guidance.

Check 04

Game-Day Preparation

Review uniforms, equipment, check-in, referee credit, game cards, reporting and safety procedures.

MatchTrak schedules are a later step. Competition names and reservation links are added after the seasonal schedules are finalized. Do not use an old MatchTrak competition name for the new season.

Respect the Call

Region 154 Does Not Tolerate Referee Abuse

Referees must be treated with respect before, during and after every match. Coaches, players and spectators may not intimidate, threaten, insult or aggressively confront any member of the referee crew.

Concerns about decisions must be handled through the approved Region process—not through an argument with the referee crew. Youth referees require additional patience, protection and adult support.

When an interaction becomes unsafe, stop the confrontation, seek a Field Marshal or Region representative and report the event to referee leadership.

Common Referee-Program Questions

Referee Central FAQ

Where should a completely new adult volunteer begin?

Begin on Become a Referee. It explains the adult application, compliance, training and first-assignment process.

Where should a referee under 18 begin?

Begin on Youth Referees. A parent or guardian must complete the separate youth-volunteer registration process.

Where are current referee courses listed?

Current courses, meetings, locations and AYSOU instructions are maintained on Training & Events.

Where are MatchTrak schedules listed?

Current competition links will be maintained on Game Day Tools after the seasonal MatchTrak schedules are published.

Does NOCRA replace Region 154 volunteer referees?

No. Region 154 continues to rely on certified AYSO volunteers for Assistant Referee and eligible Center Referee assignments.

May an AYSO volunteer serve as the Center Referee?

Appropriately certified referees may reserve eligible Center positions according to the current competition and assignment procedures.

Where are current referee-point totals listed?

Seasonal totals and team requirements belong on the current Referee & Volunteer Point Program page, not on Referee Central.

Where can I review the current Laws?

Rules & Learning contains the current annual update, official Laws links, Build-Out Line guidance and learning resources.

How do I request a mentor?

Contact the RRA and explain your current badge, experience, upcoming assignment and development goal.

How do I pursue paid referee assignments?

Review Advancement & Paid Refereeing. The U.S. Soccer, Cal South and NOCRA pathway is separate from AYSO volunteer certification.

What should I do about referee abuse?

Seek immediate support from the referee crew, Field Marshal or Region representative and report the event through the approved referee-protection process.

Who should I contact when I am unsure?

Email the Regional Referee Administrator for help identifying the correct process or resource.
